Find an old favorite.
Make a new mix.

Browse by album, artist, genre, or track. Edit titles, artwork, and ratings. Bring an album into focus, or gather your next listen into playlists and Smart Playlists.

Listen while you organize

Play supported tracks with desktop media controls. Open Synesthesia for music-reactive visuals.

Choose how edits save

Library edits save automatically by default. Turn on Draft all changes to review and save them together.

Decide what goes.
See what changes.

Choose media folders on your computer, select the albums and tracks you want, then review additions, updates, and removals before you sync. Removal candidates start unchecked.

Bring your files.
Keep the details.

Device-aware conversion prepares compatible audio and video. Artwork travels with your library. Optional acoustic matching helps recognize recordings across format and metadata changes.

Media inspection and conversion need separately installed FFmpeg and FFprobe. Chromaprint is optional. Media-tool setup

Made for the iPod
you still carry.

iOpenPod 2 targets filesystem-accessible iPods. Connect one that your computer can mount as a readable, writable drive.

iPod & iPod classic
Full-size generations 1–5.5 and the classic family
iPod mini
1st and 2nd generation
iPod nano
1st–7th generation

Features depend on the model. Hardware validation continues during development. iPod shuffle, iPod touch, and iPhone are outside the 2.0 target. DRM-protected media is not supported.
