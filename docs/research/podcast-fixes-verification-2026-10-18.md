# Podcast fixes verified against four virtual iPods

The changes in [ADR-0149](../adr/0149-author-podcasts-from-finder-capture-evidence.md)
were exercised through production Podcast Sync on 2026-10-18. This repeats the
[before-change comparison](podcast-virtual-comparison-2026-10-18.md) with fresh
isolated virtual volumes and the same 15 Episodes from three shows.

The original captured MP3 bytes supplied enclosure responses. Saved publisher RSS
was parsed again by the updated feed client, rather than manually assigning new
metadata. Planning, inspection, artwork preparation, native serialization, signing,
Storage publication, and readback used iOpenPod's implementations. The empty
firmware dataset shells and virtual identities follow the original experiment.
No physical iPod or original capture was modified.

## Integrity and limits

All 60 virtual media files are byte-identical to their captured counterparts. The
four original database SHA-256 values are unchanged. All four generated primary
databases reproduce their bytes through unchanged parsing/serialization. Classic
HASH58, Nano 5 HASH72, and Nano 5's Locations checksum book verify successfully.

Private reproducibility artifacts live under
`.scratch/podcast-fixed-comparison/`: `generate.py`, `refresh_inputs.py`,
`inspect_fixed.py`, `verify.py`, the saved inputs, per-model run records,
`fixed-findings.json`, and `verified-manifest.json`. The four populated volumes are
under `devices-v2/`. The original comparison remains available under
`.scratch/podcast-virtual-comparison/`.

Physical firmware playback remains untested. In particular, this establishes valid
show references without proving that they alone explain Classic's “Root” display.
Other observed differences such as Track header layouts, fresh playback state,
Apple's duplicated album rows, Sort Album, and lyrics remain outside this change.

## Podcast playlist settings follow-up

The subsequent playlist-settings fix was checked through public Library preparation
and the application resource-capture path. New special Podcasts Playlists now use
sort order 3, MHOD 102, and caller-captured nonzero creation/modification dates in
both dataset counterparts. Affected retained Playlists gain missing settings and
an unset sort order; existing settings and nondefault sort orders survive.

The MHOD 102 payload is identical in all seven Apple Podcast playlist counterparts:
332 bytes, SHA-256
`f830c7518c97b0d1a1430c956c03603274c53f0cba6303015306fc00aaf5304a`.
An extracted fixture under `tests/fixtures/writing/podcast-playlist-finder.json`
checks the generated bytes independently of the authoring constant. Its nonzero
little-endian words are 1 at byte 0, 1 at byte 8, 4 at byte 76, and 120 at byte 140;
no meaning is assigned to those opaque words.

Regression coverage checks flat/grouped copies, iTunesDB/iTunesCDB, device offset
conversion, preservation of custom settings with unknown trailing bytes, retained
creation dates, updated modification dates, and unchanged writes with a later
clock value. The application test checks one captured time for both counterparts.
The populated virtual volumes above remain evidence from the earlier run; this
follow-up does not rewrite those volumes or claim physical firmware verification.

The follow-up validation passed 1,043 tests across Library preparation, SQLite,
MHOD parsing/writing, application resource capture, and Podcast Sync execution.
Mypy, Ruff lint/format, Rumdl, and the locked dependency check also pass.

## Repository validation

The full 6,025-test run completed with 6,003 passes, 14 platform-specific skips,
and eight failures. Four assertions still expected the previous Podcast metadata;
four exposed diagnostics using the new GUID where a download URL was required.
Those were corrected and all eight passed their targeted rerun. Additional focused
runs cover the new advisory-whitespace case, timed artwork, feed identity recovery,
and the explicit collection types requested for Pylance diagnostics.

The locked dependency check, Ruff formatting and lint, Rumdl, and Mypy all pass.
