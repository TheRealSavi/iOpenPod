# Classic timed-artwork physical readback

Research date: **2026-10-11**. A user reported that their connected Classic,
identified as generation 6.5, continued displaying the first image after an authored
15-second transition on the **normal Now Playing screen**. The expanded artwork
view was not tested. The reported artwork count was one. This investigation
captured the saved files read-only; it did **not** observe playback, reproduce the
screen behavior independently, or test a modified database on the device.

The readback confirms that two distinct images and their 0/15,000 ms groups were
saved. It does not establish why this firmware failed to switch. In particular,
the earlier [Video 5G emulator result](artwork-timing-experiments.md) must not be
generalized into physical Classic compatibility.

## Capture and identity

Storage discovery found one mounted iTunesDB. All filesystem observations used
`Storage.open_session(..., access=AccessMode.READ_ONLY)` and bounded snapshot or
range reads. No Application Layer selection, reconciliation, repair, save, or eject
workflow ran. Copies, reports, audio-container metadata, and preview images remain
in the ignored Host directory
`.scratch/diagnostics/timed-artwork-physical-20261011T144034Z/`. Source fingerprints
for all five captured database/device-metadata files were rechecked after capture
and again around the later range reads; they remained unchanged.
See local `capture-manifest.json` and `range-inspection.json`.

The saved SysInfo identifies `xB565`; the saved SysInfoExtended identifies `MB565`.
The current [Device Registry catalog][catalog] associates MB565 with the 120 GB
Classic generation 6.5. SysInfo contains `visibleBuildID: 1.62`. Its authority
record attributes this value to an earlier hardware observation dated October 6,
2026. **This was not a fresh firmware query, and `1.62` is not independently
established here as the running Apple OS release.** Physical serials and library
identities remain in the private capture.
See local `capture-manifest.json` and the captured device files.

Crucially, the authority record marks **SysInfoExtended as generated by
`device_registry` on September 15, 2026**. Its AlbumArt list contains 1055, 1060,
1061, and 1068; it contains no `ChapterImageSpecs` or `ChapterImageSpecs2` and no
per-format media masks. This absence describes the generated file, not a native
firmware capability response. It cannot establish that the device lacks separate
chapter-artwork formats. See local `capture-manifest.json` and the captured
authority record.

## Saved database and image evidence

The strict local parsers read 235 Tracks and 643 ArtworkDB image items. Exactly one
image item has nonzero MHNI time keys. Fifteen Tracks directly reference it; one
also matches its reverse Track link. Each of those MHIT records has a `0x270`-byte
header, `artwork_count = 1`, `has_artwork = 1`, `artwork_size = 307200`, and
`media_type = 1` (ordinary audio). None contains MHOD type 17 chapter metadata.
The shared MHII has nine children, including eight thumbnail containers, and
`source_image_size = 307200`. See local `database-inspection-sanitized.json`.

The MHNI containers appear in the following physical order. Offsets and lengths are
decimal bytes in the named `_1.ithmb` file. Every range is in bounds, both MHNI
size fields agree, and all horizontal/vertical padding fields are zero.
See local `database-inspection-sanitized.json` and `range-inspection.json`.

| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0 | F1055_1.ithmb | 128 × 128 | 4358144 | 32768 |
| 2 | 0 | F1060_1.ithmb | 320 × 320 | 27238400 | 204800 |
| 3 | 0 | F1061_1.ithmb | 56 × 56 | 834176 | 6272 |
| 4 | 0 | F1068_1.ithmb | 128 × 128 | 4358144 | 32768 |
| 5 | 15000 | F1055_1.ithmb | 128 × 128 | 4751360 | 32768 |
| 6 | 15000 | F1060_1.ithmb | 320 × 320 | 29696000 | 204800 |
| 7 | 15000 | F1061_1.ithmb | 56 × 56 | 909440 | 6272 |
| 8 | 15000 | F1068_1.ithmb | 128 × 128 | 4751360 | 32768 |

The two captured payloads differ for every format. Decoding the two F1060 ranges
as RGB565 little-endian, 320 × 320 with a 640-byte row stride, produces different
RGB values in **97,943 of 102,400 pixels**. The private PNGs were also visually
reviewed and show distinct coherent images. This excludes identical saved images
as the explanation for this reported failure; it does not verify firmware loading
or selection of those ranges. See local `pixel-comparison.json` for pixel
comparison and output hashes.

The MHIT filetype describes an M4A container, which alone cannot distinguish AAC
from ALAC. Bounded reads of the `moov` box from **all fifteen referenced media
files** independently found an **`alac` sample entry and `soun` handler**. The
compressed audio payloads were not copied. Thus this physical case concerns ALAC
ordinary-audio Tracks, unlike the plain MP3 used in the successful emulator test.
See local `range-inspection.json`.

## Interpretation and next discrimination

This is evidence against a simple missing-save, reversed-group-order,
out-of-bounds-range, or identical-payload explanation. It is not evidence that
every Classic, ALAC Track, or firmware behaves the same way. No native firmware
trace or independently controlled playback observation was obtained.

The captured ArtworkDB has no F1029 format entry or reference, and its Artwork
directory has no F1029 file. Primary producer evidence distinguishes ordinary
cover formats from chapter-artwork formats, making format selection a candidate
for further testing. **Missing F1029 is not proven to cause this failure.** Codec,
media classification, chapter metadata, selection/refresh paths, and firmware-specific behavior
remain separable variables. Likewise, `artwork_count = 1` is an observed value,
not an established defect or justification for changing it.
[Producer constraints](artwork-timing-experiments.md#source-constraints).

The historical libgpod Classic chapter table contains both 1055 and 1029: **1055 is
already present in this readback**. Combined with the reported failure being limited
to normal Now Playing and the expanded view being untested, this makes a simple
"add 1029" diagnosis unjustified. [Pinned chapter table and family assignments][chapter-table].

Further physical testing should use one controlled Track and change one variable
at a time, after collecting native capability/firmware evidence where available.
This readback establishes no necessary production format/catalog/count change and contains no
claim that a proposed fix has succeeded.

## Follow-up: chapter formats and count published

On the user's subsequent request, the revised application writer was used to
prepare and publish a combined test on the same connected device. This changes
both representation coverage and count; a successful playback result would not
isolate which intervention mattered. [ADR-0139](../../adr/0139-complete-timed-artwork-formats-and-counts.md)
records the authoring policy.

The retained zero/15,000-ms groups gained one 200 × 200 RGB565LE F1029 image each.
The new `F1029_1.ithmb` is 160,000 bytes, with 80,000-byte images at offsets zero
and 80,000. All eight original thumbnail wrappers and their ranges were retained
byte-exactly. The 15 affected Tracks now reference one new shared image item and
have MHIT `artwork_count = 2`. Their other native bytes are unchanged. The previous
image item remains, with its reverse owner cleared; the other 642 image items are
byte-exact. Chapter metadata, media kinds, and ALAC files were not modified.

The private harness first required the original captured hashes and device
identity, then audited the prepared bytes, Track changes, representation changes,
file allowlist, and HASH58 signature. Publication freshly prepared the same
candidate and required its audit and all file hashes to match the reviewed
manifest. The ordinary Application Layer/Storage transaction committed exactly
three files: F1029, ArtworkDB, and iTunesDB. Readback hashes matched all three
reviewed outputs; recovery material was retained. No eject was performed.

The private review and publication manifests are under
`.scratch/diagnostics/classic-timed-prepared-20261011-retry5/` and
`.scratch/diagnostics/classic-timed-published-20261011/`. The latter records the
transaction recovery path. The harness is `.scratch/prepare_classic_timed_artwork.py`.

**Playback outcome: the user reports no switch on normal Now Playing.** Database
verification and a committed save did not establish physical timed-artwork
support. The user subsequently added chapters through Metadata Editor on the
same ALAC Tracks and reported that those chapters were not visible either.
The [chapter follow-up](classic-chapter-encoding.md) records the new readback,
the missing terminal chapter atom in those records, and the limits of that
observation. The expanded artwork view has not been reported as tested.

After the chapter-writing fix, the user reported that chapters now work while
the later artwork still does not appear. This separates the chapter-navigation
defect from the remaining artwork failure; it does not establish a media-kind
restriction or universal Classic behavior.

`database-inspection-private.json`, `database-inspection-sanitized.json`,

bounded `moov` boxes, and two decoded PNGs. Track identities/titles and media paths

inspection scripts are under `.scratch/` as `capture_connected_timed_artwork.py`,
`inspect_connected_timed_artwork.py`, `capture_timed_artwork_ranges.py`, and
